# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Added **Raw Log Resolution Enhancement** (`quick_pp/conditioning.py`, `POST /workspace/wells/{well_name}/resolution_enhancement`, `InspectorQaqc.svelte`, `LogTrackCanvas.svelte`, `CrossplotCanvas.svelte`, `tests/test_conditioning_deconv.py`, `tests/test_conditioning_backend.py`) implementing 1D constrained deconvolution and inter-sensor resolution harmonization of `GR`, `NPHI`, and `RHOB`. A bounded Huber-regularized inverse problem with an exact adjoint, edge-replicate padding, Morozov discrepancy auto-tuning, and a rolling bulk-volume tether deconvolves all three nuclear logs onto a common target vertical resolution, eliminating the inter-sensor footprint mismatch that scatters Thomas-Stieber laminated points into unphysical boomerang loops. Emits additive `GR_ENH`/`NPHI_ENH`/`RHOB_ENH`, forward-parity `*_RECON`, and `DECONV_WEIGHT` curves; raw logs are never modified. Because it conditions raw logs before any non-linear transform, it lives in the **Data & QAQC** step next to badhole flagging and environmental corrections rather than inside a single module. An explicit `use_enhanced_logs` toggle feeds the harmonized curves into Thomas-Stieber, lithology, porosity, and saturation (permeability follows through the PHIT/VCLAY it consumes), and the new **Resolution Enhanced vs. Raw** crossplot renders raw, enhanced, and forward-reconstruction traces against depth. Enhanced logs can also be overlaid on the GR and neutron-density tracks. The MAD noise-estimate cap (`noise_cap_mult`, default 3.0× the tool noise floor) is exposed as a tunable solver parameter in the API and the QAQC inspector.

## 0.3.5 (2026-11-09)
- Added a **Multi-Well Batch Evaluation** workbench to preview and compare single-well lithology, porosity, permeability, and saturation calculations by well and zone before applying selected results. Preview calculations are isolated from saved data, profiles are loaded on demand, apply validates an input fingerprint to detect stale results, and parameter snapshots are saved per zone.
- Added **Missing Data Imputation** and a depth-binned **Missing Data Matrix** to single- and multi-well QA/QC. Supported methods include mean, median, most frequent, forward/backward fill, depth-based linear interpolation, KNN, and iterative imputation; results can be saved as `_IMP` curves or replace originals while preserving full-well `_ORI` backups.
- Expanded log import support to **DLIS and LIS79 (`.lis`)** files, added uploader depth-unit conversion controls for LAS data, and improved upload reconciliation for wells sharing a UWI and cases where an upload completed without appearing in the project.
- Improved depth and curve visualization with a log-depth QA/QC plot, multi-well stick-plot performance updates, clearer crossplot selection, mobility track support, and improved curve/depth alignment in plotting and workbench views.
- Enhanced **hydrocarbon and borehole-environment corrections**, including mud-filtrate and pressure/temperature-based fluid-property inputs, revised neutron-density correction defaults, and related safeguards and curve handling.
- Refined saturation analysis with additional crossplots and improved B/Qv fitting; updated multi-mineral inversion with mica support and revised mineral constraints, plus clearer plotting of constructed porosity for QA/QC.
- Improved single-well and multi-well workflow interactions, including log-based stress estimation in core preparation, interval/lasso selection, and well deletion handling.
